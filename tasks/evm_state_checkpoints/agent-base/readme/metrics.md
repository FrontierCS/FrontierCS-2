- `committed_tps`: Geometric mean over the train Spectrum workloads of the candidate's median
  committed transactions per second.
- `checkpoint_create_ns`: Median over train seeds of the candidate's first checkpoint-micro
  creation time, in nanoseconds per checkpoint.
- `checkpoint_restore_ns`: Median over train seeds of the candidate's first checkpoint-micro
  restoration time, in nanoseconds.
- `retained_bytes_per_checkpoint`: Maximum over train seeds of the candidate's first
  checkpoint-micro retained bytes per live checkpoint.
