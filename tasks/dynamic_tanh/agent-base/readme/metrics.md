- `top1_accuracy_pct`: Candidate top-1 validation accuracy after the workload's training run, in
  percentage points; mean over workloads.
- `training_latency_ms`: Candidate median time of the 16-step fp16 training block, in
  milliseconds; mean over workloads.
- `inference_latency_ms`: Candidate median time of the 16-batch forward-only inference block, in
  milliseconds; mean over workloads.
