- `mean_recall`: Candidate recall averaged over the train operating points.
- `mean_ratio`: Candidate overall (mean rankwise inner-product) ratio averaged over the train
  operating points.
- `mean_kratio`: Candidate kth-result inner-product ratio averaged over the train operating
  points (diagnostic).
- `mean_time_ms`: Candidate query time averaged over the train operating points, in
  milliseconds.
- `mean_candidates`: Candidate verified candidates per query averaged over the train operating
  points.
