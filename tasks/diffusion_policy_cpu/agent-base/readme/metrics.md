- `dev_mean_episode_max_reward`: Mean episode-maximum reward over all train episodes (0 to 1).
- `dev_episode_success_rate`: Fraction of train episodes that reach the original environment
  success.
- `dev_mean_predict_action_seconds`: Total predict_action time divided by the number of calls,
  in seconds.
- `dev_total_workload_wall_seconds`: Sum over the train workloads of each workload's median
  episode-process seconds.
