- `geomean_speedup`: Geometric mean over the train workloads of the median paired speedup over
  the naive search (unitless).
- `search_latency_ms`: Candidate median search latency per train workload, in milliseconds;
  sorted, without workload labels.
- `iso_recall`: Candidate worst timed iso-result recall per train workload; sorted, without
  workload labels.
- `nn_recall`: Candidate worst timed nearest-neighbor recall@k per train workload; sorted,
  without workload labels.
