- `geomean_speedup`: Geometric mean over the train workloads of the median paired
  naive/candidate time ratio (unitless).
- `latency_ms`: Candidate median call latency per train workload, in milliseconds; sorted,
  without workload labels.
- `worst_ball_recall`: Candidate worst timed ball-recall per train workload; sorted, without
  workload labels.
- `worst_exact_recall`: Candidate worst timed exact recall per train workload; sorted, without
  workload labels.
