- `mean_recall`: Arithmetic mean over the train workloads of the candidate's recall at 50.
- `mean_kratio`: Arithmetic mean over the train workloads of the candidate's last-result
  inner-product ratio.
- `mean_time_ms`: Arithmetic mean over the train workloads of the candidate's mean query time,
  in milliseconds.
- `mean_candidates`: Arithmetic mean over the train workloads of the candidate's distinct
  verified candidates per query.
