- `mean_resident_bytes`: Mean over the train workloads of the candidate's maximum retained
  allocation, in bytes.
- `mean_build_ns`: Mean over the train workloads of the candidate's median construction time, in
  nanoseconds.
- `mean_lookup_ns`: Mean over the train workloads of the candidate's median time per lookup, in
  nanoseconds.
- `mean_effective_fpr`: Mean over the train workloads of the candidate's worst empirical
  false-positive probability.
- `space_overhead_defined_workloads`: Number of train workloads whose candidate space-overhead
  point is defined (false-positive rate strictly between 0 and 1).
